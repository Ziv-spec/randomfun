Also procrastination is not the same as addiction. Addiction is treated in a different way. The way to know if you are addicted is by trying to quit and seeing how it goes. If after 10 minutes or less you have a strong sensation maybe closer to a need, to return to whatever you are trying to quit, I believe my friend you are addicted. If on the other hand you feel no such need, but struggle non-the-less with procrastination then I might help. 

Addiction is the strong inclination to do, use, or indulge in something repeatedly.
Procrastination is the action of delaying or postponing something. 

You might not do something because you can't motivate yourself to do it. Or you might want to do something addictive instead of the thing you actualy are trying to do.

Procrastination is the lack of energy or belief of lack of energy to do something.
